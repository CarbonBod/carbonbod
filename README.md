## What is CarbonBod?

CarbonBod is building the carbon registry for West Africa — inspired by Ghana's GoldBod model of national commodity authority, applied to the region's largest untapped asset: **verified carbon**.

### The problem

- West Africa needs **$294 billion** in climate finance but accesses a fraction of it
- Existing registries (Verra, Gold Standard) cost $150-300K and 18-36 months per project — pricing out African developers entirely
- The 2023 integrity scandal showed the world that *estimated* credits are broken — 90% of some rainforest credits were phantom
- ECOWAS just validated a regional carbon market framework (Aug 2026) with no technical infrastructure behind it yet

### The CarbonBod way

| Old registries | CarbonBod |
|---|---|
| Annual auditor visits | Continuous IoT sensor monitoring |
| Estimated baselines | Measured data, on-chain |
| 18-36 month registration | Weeks, digital-first |
| $150-300K entry cost | Accessible to smallholder cooperatives |
| Opaque paper credits | Tamper-proof digital issuance |
| Developer-hostile | Built for West African realities |

### Architecture

1. **MRV Network** — IoT stove sensors, satellite deforestation feeds, smart meters feeding continuous verification data
2. **Registry Core** — Project registration, credit issuance, corresponding adjustments (Article 6.2 / ITMO compliant)
3. **Settlement Layer** — Digital credit custody, transparent retirement, mobile-money payout rails (MTN MoMo)
4. **Trust Mark** — Every CarbonBod credit carries the *"Webara Verified"* data-integrity seal
